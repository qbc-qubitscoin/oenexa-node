# Government & Partner Integration (Phase 27)

**Version**: v1.0 | **Phase**: 27 | **Status**: Pilot engagements with 3 government entities

## Overview

Phase 27 formalises integrations with government entities, central banks, multilateral institutions (IMF, World Bank), and strategic enterprise partners, leveraging all prior OENEXA infrastructure.

## Integration Tracks

### 1. Government
- **National Digital Identity Bridge**: OenexaID DIDs recognized by eIDAS 2.0 (EU) and equivalent frameworks globally.
- **Public Procurement**: Government contracts recorded on-chain, utilizing OenexaID to verify bidders.
- **Social Benefit Distribution**: CBDC (Phase 19) used for targeted, programmable social payments directly to citizens' wallets.

### 2. Central Banks
- **mCBDC (Multi-CBDC) Interoperability Hub**: Connecting multiple sovereign CBDCs for cross-border settlement in seconds.
- **BIS Project Nexus Compatibility**: OENEXA serving as a robust connecting network for global payments.

### 3. Enterprise Partners
- **ERP Integration**: SAP/Oracle connectors for on-chain invoice settlement via ISO 20022 messaging (Phase 20).
- **Supply Chain**: OENEXA providing an immutable, quantum-safe supply chain audit trail.
- **ESG Reporting**: Automated ESG data pipelines from chain history to sustainability reports (GRI, TCFD standards) using Phase 5 and Phase 26 data.

## Deployment Models
- Support for both public mainnet enterprise adoption and Phase 29 Sovereign sub-networks for sensitive government data.
